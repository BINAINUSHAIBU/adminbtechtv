INTRODUCTION TO THE QUESTIONNAIRE

Dear Respondent,

You are invited to participate in a research study examining the relationship between digital transformation and efficiency in human resource administration at the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria, Enugu Zonal Office. This study is being conducted in partial fulfilment of the requirements for the award of a Master’s degree at Miva Open University.

Your honest and candid responses are essential to the integrity of this research. The questionnaire is organized into seven sections and should take approximately fifteen to twenty minutes to complete. There are no right or wrong answers; your personal experience and perception are what matter most.

Please note that your participation is entirely voluntary and your responses will be treated with strict confidentiality. No personally identifying information will be published or disclosed in any form.

Kindly tick (✓) or circle the response option that most accurately reflects your experience or opinion.

Thank you sincerely for your time and cooperation.
